# Used cars

## Fields to pull per listing

Price (CLP), make, model, version, year, km, transmission, fuel, region and commune, seller type (private, dealer, brand-certified), plate if shown, number of owners, photos count, listing age, URL.

## Red flags

Flag, do not hide: a flagged listing may still be shortlisted if the recommendation says why.

- **Price well under the comp.** More than ~15% below the median of the same make, model and year band is a question, not a bargain.
- **Km that does not fit the year.** Roughly 12,000 to 20,000 km a year is normal; far below suggests a rolled-back odometer, far above means heavy use.
- **No plate in the photos or text, or "papeles al día" without detail.**
- **Several owners, "prenda", "multas" or "sin revisión técnica" mentioned or dodged.**
- **Seller pushes off-platform payment, a deposit before a visit, or an overseas seller.**
- **Same photos under another seller or another price** (a cloned listing).
- **Listing very old** with a price that never dropped: an inflated ask.

## Checks to hand the user before paying

Say these in the recommendation; the agent cannot do them.

- Certificate of current annotations from the Registro Civil (liens, thefts, prohibitions) for the plate.
- Valid revisión técnica and permiso de circulación.
- A mechanical inspection by an independent shop.
- Meet in a public place and pay only through a traceable transfer once the transfer paperwork is signed.
